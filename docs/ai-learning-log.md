# AI-Assisted Learning Log

The challenge requires each engineer to record at least two learning cycles. Do not fill this log with invented experiments or results. Complete each entry after you personally run the experiment and verify the outcome.

## Learning cycle template

### Cycle [number] — Engineer [A or B]

- **Question asked:** 
- **AI explanation/advice:** Summarize the advice in your own words.
- **Prediction before testing:** What did you expect to happen, and why?
- **Experiment:** Record the code/input/command you tried.
- **Observed result:** Paste the relevant output or describe the behavior.
- **Verification:** Explain how you checked whether the advice was correct.
- **Final decision:** Accepted, adjusted, or rejected, with a reason.
- **What I learned:** Explain what you can now describe without AI.
